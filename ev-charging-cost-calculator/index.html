How to use this EV charging calculator

Enter your EV battery capacity, current battery percentage, desired charging percentage and electricity price.

The calculator estimates how many units of electricity your vehicle will consume and the approximate charging cost.

Calculation formula

Battery Energy Needed = Battery Capacity × (Target % − Current %) ÷ 100
Grid Energy = Battery Energy Needed ÷ Charging Efficiency
Charging Cost = Grid Energy × Electricity Price

Example

Suppose your EV has a 75 kWh battery and you want to charge it from 20% to 80%.

The battery needs 45 kWh of energy. With 90% charging efficiency, approximately 50 kWh may be drawn from the grid. At ₹8 per unit, the estimated cost is about ₹400.

Important note

Actual electricity consumption can vary because of charging losses, temperature, charger efficiency, battery management and other factors. Your electricity bill may also use different tariff slabs or fixed charges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to charge an EV?

It depends mainly on battery capacity, the amount of energy being added and your electricity tariff.

What is charging efficiency?

Charging efficiency accounts for energy lost between the electricity supply and the energy stored in the battery.

Does the calculator include charging losses?

Yes. The efficiency field allows you to account for charging losses.
