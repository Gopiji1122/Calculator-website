What is Battery Capacity?

Battery capacity is the amount of energy your EV battery can store. It is usually shown in kWh (kilowatt-hours).

For example, if your EV has a 75 kWh battery, enter 75.

You can usually find this specification in your vehicle's manual, manufacturer's website or EV specification sheet.

Current battery percentage.
What is Current Battery Level?

This is the battery percentage currently shown on your EV dashboard.

For example, if your EV currently has 20% battery, enter 20.

Enter a value between 0% and 100%.

Battery percentage you want to reach.
What is Target Battery Level?

This is the battery percentage you want your EV to reach after charging.

For example, if you want to charge from 20% to 80%, enter 80 here.

The target level must be higher than the current battery level.

₹ per kWh (₹ per unit)
What is Electricity Price?

This is the price you pay for one unit of electricity. In electricity billing, 1 unit = 1 kWh.

For example, if your effective electricity cost is ₹8 per unit, enter 8.

Your actual electricity cost may vary depending on your electricity provider, tariff slab, fixed charges, taxes and other applicable charges.

Percentage of grid electricity effectively stored in the battery.
What is Charging Efficiency?

Charging efficiency represents how much of the electricity drawn from the grid is actually stored in the EV battery.

Some energy is lost as heat and through the charging system.

Don't know the value? Leave it at 90%. This is a reasonable default estimate.

Actual efficiency can vary depending on the EV, charger, temperature and charging conditions.

Charger power in kW.
What is Charger Power?

Charger power tells you how much electrical power the charger can provide at a given time.

It is measured in kW.

Common AC home chargers may be around 3.3 kW, 7.2 kW or 11 kW, while some DC fast chargers can be much more powerful.

How to Use the EV Charging Cost Calculator

Enter your EV's battery capacity, current battery level, target battery level, electricity price, charging efficiency and charger power. Then select Calculate Charging Cost.

How is EV charging cost calculated?

First, the calculator determines how much energy needs to be stored in the battery.

Battery Energy Needed = Battery Capacity × (Target % − Current %) ÷ 100

Because some electricity is lost during charging, the calculator then adjusts the required energy using the charging efficiency.

Grid Energy = Battery Energy Needed ÷ Charging Efficiency

Finally:

Charging Cost = Grid Energy × Electricity Price

Example

Suppose your EV has a 75 kWh battery and you want to charge it from 20% to 80%.

With an electricity price of ₹8 per unit and charging efficiency of 90%, the calculator estimates the electricity required from the grid and the approximate charging cost.

Important Note

This calculator provides an estimate. Actual charging consumption can vary because of charging losses, battery temperature, charger efficiency, vehicle electronics, battery conditioning and other factors.

Your actual electricity bill may also differ because electricity tariffs can include slab rates, fixed charges, taxes and other applicable charges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to charge an EV?

The cost depends mainly on the amount of electricity required, your electricity price and charging losses. Enter your values above to calculate an estimate.

What does 1 unit of electricity mean?

One unit of electricity is equal to 1 kWh.

Does charging efficiency affect the result?

Yes. If charging efficiency is below 100%, the EV needs to draw more electricity from the grid than the energy ultimately stored in the battery.

Can I use this calculator for a home EV charger?

Yes. Enter the rated power of your home charger, such as 3.3 kW, 7.2 kW or another applicable value.

Is the charging time exact?

No. It is an estimate based on the charger power and calculated grid energy. Real charging time can vary because charging power may change during the charging session, particularly at higher battery levels.
