Land Area Calculator India

Convert common land measurements including square feet, square metre, gaj, acre, hectare, bigha and biswa.

1. Enter Area

Enter the land area you want to convert.
Choose the unit used for your entered area. Bigha and Biswa require a regional conversion because their size varies by location.
Bigha and Biswa can vary by state, district and local convention. Select the convention that matches your area. These are commonly reported reference values, not a legal land-record definition.
Enter the local value of exactly 1 Bigha in square metres (m²). Example: if your local land record says 1 Bigha = 2,529.29 m², enter 2529.29.

Use square metres (m²) for your custom Bigha value.
