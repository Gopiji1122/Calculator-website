Electricity Bill Calculator India

Estimate your electricity bill using your state, electricity provider and monthly electricity consumption. You don't need to know technical electricity terms to use this calculator.

Why do we need your state?

Electricity tariffs in India are not the same everywhere. Your state helps us identify the electricity regulatory authority and applicable distribution company.

We will use the applicable tariff information instead of using one generic electricity rate for all of India.

Don't know your provider? You can usually find its name or logo on your electricity bill.
What is an electricity provider / DISCOM?

DISCOM means Distribution Company. It is the company or utility that supplies electricity to your home or business.

Different DISCOMs can have different applicable tariff structures.

If you don't know the name, don't worry. We will provide an option to continue where possible.

What is Connection Type?

This tells us how the electricity connection is being used.

For a normal household, select Home / Domestic.

Shops, factories, farms and other users may have different tariff categories.

Enter the number of units shown on your electricity bill.
What is an electricity unit?

One unit of electricity is equal to 1 kWh (kilowatt-hour).

Look at your electricity bill for terms such as Units Consumed, Consumption or kWh.

Example: If your bill shows 250 units consumed, enter 250.

Enter in kW if you know it. Otherwise, leave it blank.
What is Sanctioned Load?

It is the electrical load approved for your connection by your electricity provider.

You may find it on your electricity bill under terms such as Sanctioned Load, Connected Load or similar wording.

Don't know it? No problem. Leave this field blank.

Advanced information is optional. We will only use it when the selected tariff requires it.

How to Use This Electricity Bill Calculator

Select your state, electricity provider and connection type, then enter the number of electricity units consumed during the billing period.

Why does the calculator ask for your state?

Electricity tariffs in India are determined through the applicable regulatory framework and can vary by state, distribution company and consumer category. State regulators publish tariff orders for their respective jurisdictions.

Do I need to know my sanctioned load?

No. For the normal calculation, we want to keep the calculator simple. Sanctioned load will be an optional advanced field where it is relevant to the applicable tariff.

Why can my actual bill be different?

An electricity bill can contain several components besides energy consumption. Depending on the applicable tariff, these can include fixed charges, adjustments, duties, subsidies or other charges.

Therefore, our calculator will show an estimated bill and clearly identify the tariff source and effective period used for the calculation.

Tariff Information

We will use official tariff orders and regulatory sources wherever possible. Tariff information will be connected to the calculator after it has been verified.
