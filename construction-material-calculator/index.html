Construction Material Calculator

Estimate basic construction materials for concrete, brick walls and plaster. Enter your measurements and choose the material assumptions that match your work.

Concrete Material Calculator ?

Estimate uses dry-volume factor 1.54 and cement density about 1440 kg/m³. One 50 kg cement bag is treated as about 0.0347 m³.

Brick Wall Material Calculator ?

Brick quantity is estimated from wall volume ÷ brick-with-mortar volume. Mortar is estimated from total wall volume minus actual brick volume.

Plaster Material Calculator ?

Estimate uses dry-volume factor 1.33, cement density about 1440 kg/m³ and 50 kg per cement bag.
