Solar Panel Savings Calculator

Estimate how much an on-grid solar system could reduce your electricity cost using your own system size, generation and tariff assumptions.

Solar System & Electricity Details ?

The default generation value of 120 units/kW/month is only an illustrative estimate, not an official Rajasthan/DISCOM figure. Change it to your actual or installer-provided estimate. Export credit is also user-entered and should match your applicable metering/tariff arrangement.

What is included?

Included: estimated solar generation, direct-consumption savings, export credit and combined monthly/annual savings.

Not included: system purchase price, subsidy, loan interest, maintenance, battery cost, fixed charges, demand charges, taxes, tariff-slab changes or future electricity-price changes.
