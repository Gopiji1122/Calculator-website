About this calculator

Bigha is a regional land unit. Published Indian conversion tables show different Bigha values across states, so this calculator requires a regional convention rather than assuming one national value.

The selected convention is also used when converting to or from Biswa. Where 20 Biswa = 1 Bigha is the applicable convention, the calculator uses that relationship.
