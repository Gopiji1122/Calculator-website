How to Use This Mileage Calculator

1. Odometer Method

Enter the odometer reading before and after the measurement period, then enter the fuel used or filled. The calculator determines the distance travelled and mileage.

2. Trip Method

If you already know the trip distance, select Trip Method and enter the distance and fuel used. You do not need odometer readings.

Fuel Price is Optional

You can calculate mileage without entering any fuel price. If you enter the exact price you paid per litre, the calculator also calculates total fuel cost, cost per kilometre and cost per 100 km.

Basic Formula

Mileage = Distance travelled ÷ Fuel used

Fuel cost = Fuel used × Fuel price

Cost per km = Total fuel cost ÷ Distance travelled
