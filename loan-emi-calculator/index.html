How EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1)

P = loan principal, r = monthly interest rate, and n = total number of monthly instalments. If the annual interest rate is 0%, EMI is simply the loan amount divided by the number of months.
